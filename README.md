# Customer_Shipping_dashboard
🚀 I just wrapped up a data analytics project I'm genuinely proud of — an interactive Customer Demographics Dashboard built from a real retail dataset.

The dataset had 99,457 shopping transactions from 10 shopping malls in Istanbul, spanning 2021 to 2023 — around ₺251M in total sales. My goal was simple to say but harder to do well: turn a raw CSV into something that actually answers business questions about who the customers are and how they shop.

Here's what I built it to explore:
→ How revenue splits across gender and age groups
→ Which product categories each demographic prefers
→ Payment behavior and mall-by-mall performance
→ Revenue trends month over month

One insight really stuck with me. Women drive 59.7% of total revenue — but their average basket size (₺2,525) is almost identical to men's (₺2,534). So the difference isn't that women spend more per trip; it's that they shop more often. That's the kind of finding that changes how a business thinks about its customers, and it's exactly why I love this work.

On the technical side, I built the dashboard as a single self-contained HTML file with live filters and cross-filtering, prepped the data in Python with pandas, and also documented how to rebuild the whole thing in Power BI with DAX measures — so I got to work across both worlds.

Biggest lesson? A dashboard isn't about how many charts you can fit. It's about helping someone see the story in the numbers.

Still learning, still improving — would love any feedback. 🙏
